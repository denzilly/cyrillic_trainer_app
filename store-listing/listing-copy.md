## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Learn to read Russian the fun way — one letter, one word, one streak at a time.

Cyrillic Trainer is a fast, focused way to learn the Cyrillic alphabet and
build real Russian vocabulary through quick transliteration practice. No
lengthy lessons, no clutter — just you, a letter or word, and a streak to
keep going.

HOW IT WORKS
You're shown a Cyrillic letter or word and type its Latin transliteration.
Get it right and your streak climbs; get it wrong and it resets — simple,
addictive, and genuinely useful for building recall.

SINGLE LETTER PRACTICE
Drill all 33 letters of the Russian alphabet, uppercase and lowercase. A
built-in reference grid is always one tap away if you need a reminder.

WORD PRACTICE
Practice real Russian vocabulary across categories like Greetings, Numbers,
Animals, Food, Colors, Family, the Home, Time & Calendar, Travel, and common
Verbs. See the English meaning after every answer, and use the category
picker to focus on exactly the words you want to learn.

STREAKS THAT KEEP YOU HONEST
Every correct answer builds your streak. Every mistake resets it. It's a
simple mechanic that makes practice feel like a game instead of a chore.

HIGH SCORES
Sign in with Google Play Games (optional) to submit your best streak to the
global leaderboard and see how you stack up.

WHY CYRILLIC TRAINER
- Clean, distraction-free practice loop
- Real, curated vocabulary — not machine-translated filler
- Sound effects you can toggle on or off
- No ads, no account required to start practicing
- Built specifically for learners tackling the Cyrillic alphabet from zero

Whether you're starting to learn Russian, brushing up before a trip, or just
curious about the alphabet, Cyrillic Trainer gets you reading Cyrillic
script with real confidence — one streak at a time.
