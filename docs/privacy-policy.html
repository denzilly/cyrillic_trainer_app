Privacy Policy

Cyrillic Trainer · Last updated August 12, 2026

Cyrillic Trainer ("the app") is developed by Spug Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. We built this app to be as lightweight on data as possible — there are no ads and no analytics trackers in it.

Information we collect

If you sign in to High Scores (optional): the leaderboard feature uses Google Play Games Services, Google's own SDK for game sign-in and leaderboards. If you choose to sign in:

  • Google shares your Play Games profile with the app — your display name, profile photo, and player ID.
  • The app submits your practice streak score to the leaderboard so it can show the global top 10 and your own rank.

This sign-in and the data it involves is handled by Google under its own Privacy Policy. We never see your Google account password, and we don't receive your email address or any other Google account data beyond your public Play Games profile and the score you submit.

Stored only on your device: a few preferences are saved locally so the app remembers your settings between sessions. These never leave your device and we never see them:

  • Whether sound effects are turned on or off
  • Which Word Practice categories you've selected
  • Whether you've already seen the one-time alphabet-grid tip

What we don't collect: the app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or browsing history. We don't use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics/tracking libraries of any kind.

How we use information

The only data the app handles (your Play Games profile and streak score) is used solely to power the High Scores leaderboard — so you can see how your streak compares to other players. Local preferences are used only to restore your settings the next time you open the app.

Sharing

We don't sell your data or share it with third parties, other than Google Play Games Services as described above, which is required for the leaderboard feature to function.

Your choices

  • Signing in to Play Games Services is optional — every other part of the app (letter practice, word practice, sound, help) works without it.
  • You can manage or disconnect the app's access to your Play Games profile at any time from your Google Play Games account settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored preferences from your device.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to check back occasionally.
